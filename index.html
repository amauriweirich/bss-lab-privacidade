Privacy Policy

BSS-LAB · Last updated: October 19, 2026

BSS-LAB is a private publishing tool used by a single owner to publish videos that the owner creates to the owner's own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the owner and the people the owner authorizes to manage these accounts.

Information we access

  • Access tokens granted by the account owner when connecting a Facebook Page, an Instagram professional account, a YouTube channel or a TikTok account.
  • Basic information about those accounts (name, ID, username) needed to publish to them.
  • Performance metrics of the owner's own posts (views, likes, comments, followers), used only to show reports to the owner.

We do not collect personal information about viewers, followers or any other people.

How we use it

Only to publish the owner's videos, captions and stories to the owner's own accounts, to check that each post was published correctly, and to show performance reports to the owner.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and nothing is used for advertising.

Retention

Data is kept only while the account stays connected. Disconnecting an account in BSS-LAB deletes its tokens.

Data deletion

See the data deletion instructions. You can also remove the app's access at any time in Facebook › Settings & privacy › Settings › Business integrations (or Apps and websites).

Contact

Questions about this policy: mythvaultexplained@gmail.com

Política de Privacidade

BSS-LAB · Atualizada em 19 de outubro de 2026

O BSS-LAB é uma ferramenta particular de publicação, usada por um único dono para publicar os vídeos que ele produz nos seus próprios canais do YouTube, Páginas do Facebook, contas profissionais do Instagram e contas do TikTok. Não é oferecida ao público.

O que acessamos: os tokens de acesso que o dono concede ao conectar as contas, dados básicos dessas contas (nome, ID, usuário) e as métricas das próprias publicações. Não coletamos dados de espectadores ou seguidores.

Uso: só para publicar os vídeos do dono nas contas dele, conferir se cada publicação saiu certa e mostrar relatórios ao dono.

Armazenamento: os tokens ficam cifrados no computador do dono. Nada é vendido, alugado, compartilhado ou usado para publicidade. Ao desconectar uma conta, os tokens dela são apagados.

Exclusão de dados: veja as instruções de exclusão. Contato: mythvaultexplained@gmail.com
