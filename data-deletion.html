Data Deletion Instructions

BSS-LAB · Last updated: October 19, 2026

BSS-LAB only stores the access tokens and basic account information of accounts that the owner connected. To delete them:

  1. Remove the app's access in Facebook: Settings & privacy › Settings › Business integrations (or Apps and websites), select the BSS-LAB app and click Remove. On Instagram: Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites.
  2. Or ask us to delete everything by email: mythvaultexplained@gmail.com (subject "Data deletion"). All stored tokens and account data are deleted within 30 days and we confirm by email.

Posts already published on your own Pages and accounts are not affected; you can delete them directly in Facebook or Instagram.

Exclusão de dados

Para apagar seus dados do BSS-LAB: remova o acesso do app em Configurações e privacidade › Configurações › Integrações comerciais (ou Aplicativos e sites) no Facebook, ou peça por e-mail em mythvaultexplained@gmail.com (assunto "Exclusão de dados"). Tudo é apagado em até 30 dias e confirmamos por e-mail.
